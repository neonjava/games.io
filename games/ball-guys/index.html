    How to play

    Everyone drops balls of their own colour into one jar. Two balls of the same colour and size merge into a bigger one and score points.

    A big ball that hits a smaller ball of another colour paints it your colour. The gravity changes direction every few seconds. MAGNET pulls your balls together and shoves the others away.

    Empty seats are filled by bots. Most points after 100 seconds wins.
